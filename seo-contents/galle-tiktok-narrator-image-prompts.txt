SLIDE 3 – MORNING AT GALLE FORT
================================================
Narrator:
Start your morning inside Galle Fort. Walk the old Dutch walls, visit the lighthouse, and look down at the Indian Ocean. This is the best first stop to understand Galle before going to the beaches.

Image prompt:
Vertical 9:16 wide-angle view from Galle Fort ramparts, stone walls leading toward a white lighthouse, waves crashing below, a few relaxed tourists walking, bright clear sky, travel photography style.

================================================
SLIDE 4 – IMPORTANT FORT TIP
================================================
Narrator:
Do not try to drive your tuk tuk deep inside the fort. The lanes are narrow and busy. Park just outside the main gate and explore on foot. It feels calmer and you see more hidden details.

Image prompt:
Vertical 9:16 illustration of a tuk tuk parked near the entrance of Galle Fort, sign or subtle icon showing parking area, narrow cobblestone lane leading inside, soft afternoon light, calm explorer mood.

================================================
SLIDE 5 – RIDE TO UNAWATUNA BEACH
================================================
Narrator:
Next, jump in the tuk tuk and ride about five minutes to Unawatuna. This is your easy beach stop for swimming, brunch, and relaxing under the palms before the day gets too hot.

Vertical 9:16 beach scene of Unawatuna Bay, crescent-shaped golden sand, turquoise water, palm trees leaning over the beach, a tuk tuk parked near a beach cafe, bright late-morning light.

================================================
Narrator:
From Unawatuna, head to Jungle Beach. Walk down the small jungle path to a quiet cove. It feels more hidden and peaceful, perfect if you want to escape the crowds for a while.
