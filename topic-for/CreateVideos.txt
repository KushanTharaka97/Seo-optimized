1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Optimizing websites to rank higher on search engines through content, keywords, and technical improvements.
2. Content Marketing
Creating valuable, relevant content (blogs, videos, guides) to attract, engage, and convert audiences.
3. Search Engine Marketing (SEM / PPC)
Paid advertising on search engines (e.g., Google Ads) to gain immediate visibility and traffic.
4. Keyword Research & Search Intent
Identifying what users search for and matching content to their intent (informational, navigational, transactional).
5. On-Page SEO
Optimizing individual pages using titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, and content structure.
6. Technical SEO
Improving site performance, crawlability, mobile-friendliness, page speed, and indexation.
7. Link Building & Authority
Earning high-quality backlinks to improve domain authority and search rankings.
8. Social Media Marketing
Using social platforms to promote content, build brand awareness, and drive traffic.
9. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
Improving landing pages and user journeys to convert visitors into leads or customers.
10. Analytics & Performance Tracking
Measuring results using tools like Google Analytics and Search Console to optimize strategies.
